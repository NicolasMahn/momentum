# Momentum

Track the habits you choose. Mo, a small flame, helps you keep at it.

Momentum is a habit tracker for Android. It is for the things you decide to keep up: training, a daily measurement, a checklist, a few questions about your day, minutes of reading, or staying away from alcohol or nicotine.

## Mo

Mo is a small flame who lives in the app and in a widget for your home screen. He burns brightly when you keep your routines and cools down when you let them slide. He never goes out for good: one finished task warms him again.

## Yours alone

Momentum has no account and no ads. I, the developer, never receive what you record. The app goes online for one thing: the map under a route you recorded, which comes from my own server. The privacy policy says exactly when a copy leaves the app.
